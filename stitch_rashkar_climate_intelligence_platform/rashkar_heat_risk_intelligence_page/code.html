01 / Problem Understanding
A

gricultural workers spend 8 to 12 consecutive hours outdoors engaged in intensive physical exertion. While standard public alerts rely strictly on ambient dry-bulb temperature, biological thermal strain is governed by solar radiation flux, relative humidity, wind speed, metabolic exertion rate, clothing insulation, worker age, and baseline hydration.

In 2024, extreme heat events surged across central, northern, and eastern India with over 280 heatwave days recorded. Broad public advisories failed to curb agricultural collapse because they arrived post-facto without specific work-rest pacing schedules mapped to agrarian tasks.

Clinical Principle
“The heat is environmental. The risk is strictly personal.”

Broad state forecasts tell an office worker in Pune that it is 41°C. They do not tell a 52-year-old cotton picker in Vidarbha with latent kidney strain when solar radiant load will trigger exertional heat collapse.

100M+ AGRICULTURAL WORKERS / ZERO WEARABLES / CLOUD-BASED METEOROLOGY REANALYSIS.

Smallholders and landless agrarian field laborers work without artificial shade, hydration telemetry, or wearable sensors. Providing wearable monitors across 100 million workers is economically unviable and operationally fragile.

Rashkar shifts the entire burden into the cloud: mathematically extracting physiological wet-bulb globe temperature from open satellite grids and translating it into hyper-local phone dispatch.
